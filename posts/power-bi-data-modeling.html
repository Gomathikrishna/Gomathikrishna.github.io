POWER BI · DATA MODELING

From one table to a data model in Power BI

When you import a single wide table into Power BI, it may include transactions, customers, products and dates in the same place. A data model separates these concepts to make reporting clearer.

1. Identify the grain

Ask what each row represents: one sale, one invoice line, or one customer? The answer determines the grain of your fact table.

2. Separate facts and dimensions

Keep numeric events such as sales amount and quantity in a fact table. Move descriptive attributes into dimensions such as Customer, Product and Date.

3. Create relationships

Connect dimension keys to the corresponding fact keys, typically using one-to-many relationships. Check that dimension keys are unique.

4. Validate your measures

Test totals by product, customer and time period. Confirm that filters behave as expected before building visuals.
