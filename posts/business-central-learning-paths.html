DYNAMICS 365 BUSINESS CENTRAL

Choosing your Business Central learning path

Business Central learners can approach the product from business processes, technical customization or a combination of both.

Functional consultant

Focus on finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, configuration and business requirements. Practice in a sandbox environment.

Technical / developer

Learn AL, extensions, events, APIs, debugging and deployment fundamentals.

Techno-functional

Combine process knowledge with customization and integrations. Start by understanding standard functionality before extending it.

How to choose

Select a track based on the role you want, then build small projects that demonstrate practical skills.
